# Ignacio Montenegro

I build useful internet things, help people actually use AI, and occasionally make playing cards.

I am a founder and Commercial Engineering student at Universidad de los Andes in Santiago, Chile. Most of what I do sits somewhere between AI, community, product, education, growth, magic, and human behavior. I like tools that make people feel more capable, not more confused.

I never learned how to switch my curiosity off, so I build with it instead.

## How I Work

I like learning by making things real. A rough prototype, a user conversation, or a broken workflow usually teaches me more than a long abstract plan.

I also treat AI agents as coworkers, not search boxes. Most of what I build now starts as a conversation with one.

## What I Am Looking For

I want to meet people and teams working on AI tools, AI safety, product adoption, education, community, user research, growth, or strange new workflows that make people faster and more capable. I am especially drawn to high-agency, AI-native builders working on unusual, ambitious things.

I do not fit perfectly into one box, and I am not trying to. I am most useful where building, thinking, teaching, and getting things in front of real users all overlap.
